# End-to-end utility experiment (#128) — results

**Date:** 2026-08-27
**Critic:** LLMCritic (DeepSeek, `deepseek-chat`) when a key is set; else offline
**Corpus:** 8 fixed physics facts (deterministic keyword+value oracle)

## What this measures

Every prior validation used synthetic fault injection (§8) or extracted
sentences (corroboration v2/v3). Here the claims are **generated by a live LLM**
(DeepSeek), then independently labelled correct/incorrect by a deterministic
oracle (keyword+value matchers, NOT the critic — no circularity), then routed
through the real ISNAD pipeline (chain grading → critic → decision matrix).

The ground-truth oracle passed 12/12 hand-checked cases, including the hard
paraphrases ("neither created nor destroyed" vs "conserved") and hallucinated
values ("five hundred million m/s").

## Result (live LLM, temperature 0.7, n=3 per topic)

| Condition | Served | Errors | Served-error |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ISNAD-gated** | 12 | 0 | **0.0%** |
| Ungated | 24 | 12 | 50.0% |

The gate served only the correct in-corpus restatements (with caveat) and routed
all 12 novel-topic claims (tungsten, francium, helium, Mars) to REVIEW.

## Honest limits

- **One corpus, one model, one critic, one run.** Not a claim of general
  hallucination-detection superiority (per #71).
- The novel topics are *genuinely* out-of-corpus, so "review" is the easy,
  correct call. The hard case — a confident wrong claim *within* a covered
  domain — is not what this experiment stresses.
- The oracle is conservative: true-but-unverifiable counts as incorrect, because
  the pipeline cannot serve what it cannot verify.
- Generation needs a live key; the offline path reports the coverage ceiling
  (#126) rather than fabricating a number.

## What this adds to the paper

This is the fidelity→utility crossing: κ=0.871 proved ISNAD reproduces hadith
science; this shows the pipeline reduces served-error on *real LLM-generated*
claims. Frame it as a mechanism demonstration, not a headline benchmark.

DEEPSEEK_API_KEY=sk-... python experiments/e2e_utility/run.py --n 3
