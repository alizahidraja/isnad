| Critic | Coverage |
|---|---|

## Reading

- **The stub still serves 75%**: the DeterministicRuleCritic matches verbatim corpus facts
  and serves them, but holds the paraphrases. Coverage is **not** "0% for every critic".
- **The EmbeddingCritic is now contradiction-only** (from the #232/#233 false-positive
  tightening). It can no longer affirm CONSISTENT, so its cold-start coverage is **0%** —
  every clean claim is held for review. This is a real, deliberate trade: the critic can no
  longer bless a hallucination (0.000 false-consistent), but it also can no longer *serve*
  anything. The decision matrix holds those claims (ḥasan × UNVERIFIABLE → REVIEW).
- **The seed fix (#114) still works**: the seeded chain grades ḥasan, not ḍaʿīf — the
  bottleneck for the *affirming* critics is entirely their ability to say CONSISTENT.
- **The LLM critic is the only critic that *could* affirm CONSISTENT on paraphrase, but the
  default-ON affirmation gate withholds it** (measured WITH `DEEPSEEK_API_KEY`, still 0%).
  `gated()` downgrades CONSISTENT → UNVERIFIABLE until a valid, unexpired, re-runnable eval
  record licenses affirmation for (kind=llm, domain=physics). The repo ships **no such record**
  (an empty `data/affirmation_records/` directory), so the licence is absent by default.
- **That licence is obtainable — and the honest tension is the eval set.** The committed
  60-case eval (`experiments/critic_eval/`) has 25 contradiction cases with 0 passed as
  consistent, which *meets* the gate's numeric thresholds (≥ 25 contradiction cases,
  false-consistent rate ≤ 0.0) and would license affirmation. But that eval is curated;
  the §8 real-fault measurement is **39.1% false-consistent** on content corruption (#126),
  and G1's false-consistent analogue is 30 of 795 (3.8%). So the gate is only as good as the
  eval set that licenses it — a curated eval passes, real-world faults do not. This is the
  moat working: a critic may not bless a claim until its false-consistent rate is *measured*,
  and the honest report is that the two measurements disagree.
