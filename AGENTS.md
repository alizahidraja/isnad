# Rules for AI agents working in this repo
- The maintainer approves every change before it's made. Propose first; don't act on your own.
- No new files (docs, reports, plans, notes) unless the maintainer asks for that file.
- No emoji. No self-describing adjectives (honest, rigorous, robust, powerful).
- No issue numbers or paper section references in code comments or docstrings.
- Never claim conformity or compliance with any regulation or standard. ISNAD produces evidence; it doesn't certify.
- Never edit a published number by hand. Numbers come from committed results files and are regenerated by script.
- Never weaken or remove a stated limitation without the maintainer's approval.
- Don't use review panels or personas to generate content.
- Keep public names stable. Deprecate before removing, and only in a major version.
- Show the git diff after every edit and confirm nothing outside the requested change moved.
- Merging to main publishes a release. Only the maintainer merges.
